TRL-DRL-α framework

Bioprocess Digital Twin Deployment Readiness Toolkit

A TRL-DRL-α framework for identifying the digital twin role ceiling in bioprocesses.

This toolkit helps evaluators identify whether a bioprocess is currently best suited to offline analysis, online monitoring and soft sensing, or advisory/closed-loop digital twin deployment.

Chengwan Bai1, Rachel L Gomes1, Oliver J Fisher1* 1Food Water Waste Research Group, Faculty of Engineering, University of Nottingham, University Park, NG7 2RD, UK *Corresponding author: Oliver.Fisher2@nottingham.ac.uk

Framework Summary

TRL

Technology maturity and the feasible deployment boundary.

DRL

Data maturity and evidential confidence for the assigned role.

α

Alignment among technology, data, model, and the operational task.

Role ceiling

Current defensible DT role, not theoretical future potential.

Technology Readiness Level

TRL defines the feasible deployment boundary.

Data Readiness Level

DRL defines the confidence of the role assignment.
