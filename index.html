TRL-DRL-α framework

Bioprocess Digital Twin Deployment Readiness Toolkit

A TRL-DRL-α framework for identifying the digital twin role ceiling in bioprocesses.

This toolkit helps evaluators identify whether a bioprocess is currently best suited to offline analysis, online monitoring and operator-supervised support, or automated closed-loop optimisation or control.

Chengwan Bai1, Rachel L Gomes1, Oliver J Fisher1* 1Food Water Waste Research Group, Faculty of Engineering, University of Nottingham, University Park, NG7 2RD, UK *Corresponding author: Oliver.Fisher2@nottingham.ac.uk

Framework Summary

TRL

Technology maturity, process scale, and operating conditions covered by the assessment.

DRL band

Band C confirms availability, Band B supports R1–R2, and Band A is required for R3.

α

Eight individual alignment criteria tested against role-specific requirements.

Role ceiling

Current defensible DT role, not theoretical future potential.

Step 1

Assessment specification

Operational task and target role are required. Boundary and envelope are recommended; other context fields are optional.

Step 2

Assign readiness

Assign TRL to the integrated technical system and select the supported data-readiness evidence band.

Technology Readiness Level

TRL records maturity and the scale and conditions covered; component maturity alone is insufficient.

Data Readiness Band

Select the highest evidence band fully achieved for the specified task and boundary.

The bands are cumulative. Band A includes Bands B and C, and Band B includes Band C.

Step 3

Conditional criterion applicability

αTD2 and αTD3 enter the role mapping when their stated conditions affect the task. For R2, αMT enters the role mapping when model outputs guide process operation.

Step 4 · Alignment scoring

α Sub-Criteria

Dimension means summarise each interface. R2 uses applicable dimension means together with a minimum criterion score; R3 requires all applicable criteria to reach 4.

Assessment output

Result

Structured academic assessment aid. Requires expert judgement, site-specific validation, safety assessment, and regulatory review.
